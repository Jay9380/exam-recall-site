Jeongcheogi — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-09 · Last updated 2026-10-09

1. Information collected

None. The app collects no personal information of any kind. There is no account and no sign-in.

2. What is stored, and where

Only your study progress — which cards you have cleared and how often you missed them — and it is stored on your device alone. Nobody, the developer included, can read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Network use

Study data is never sent to any server. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

The one exception is in-app purchase. Buying or restoring Pro talks to Apple's App Store, and Apple handles the payment entirely. The developer receives no payment method or Apple Account details; the device stores a single true/false value recording whether Pro was purchased.

4. Sharing

Nothing is collected, so nothing is shared.

5. Children

No information is collected, so the app is safe at any age.

6. Content

The app contains exam terms and their definitions. No past exam question text is reproduced.
